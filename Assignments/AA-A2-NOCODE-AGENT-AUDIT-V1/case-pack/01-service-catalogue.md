### 1. Digital Confidence

- Purpose: practical use of common digital tools and online services.
- Eligibility: participants must be at least 16 years old on the workshop date.
- Schedule: Saturdays, 10:00–12:00.
- Capacity: 24 participants.
- Equipment: devices are provided; participants may bring their own laptop but do not need to.
- Fee: free.

### 2. Community Story Lab

- Purpose: create and discuss short digital stories using text, images, and audio.
- Eligibility: participants must be at least 13 years old.
- Participants aged 13–15 require consent from a parent or guardian before attendance. The parent or guardian does not need to attend.
- Schedule: Wednesdays, 16:30–18:00.
- Capacity: 20 participants.
- Equipment: shared devices are available.
- Fee: free.

### 3. Data for Decisions

- Purpose: interpret tables, charts, and public data for community projects.
- Eligibility: participants must be at least 18 years old.
- Schedule: Tuesdays, 18:00–20:00.
- Capacity: 18 participants.
- Equipment: each participant must bring a laptop capable of using a modern web browser.
- Fee: free.

### 4. Advisory Scope

### 1. Digital Confidence

- 目的：学习常用数字工具和在线服务的实际使用方法。
- 资格：参加者在活动当天必须年满 16 岁。
- 时间：星期六 10:00–12:00。
- 人数：24 人。
- 设备：机构提供设备；参加者可以自带笔记本电脑，但不是必须。

- 目的：使用文字、图片和音频创作并讨论短篇数字故事。
- 资格：参加者必须年满 13 岁。
- 13–15 岁参加者需要在参加活动前获得父母或监护人同意，但父母或监护人不需要陪同出席。
- 时间：星期三 16:30–18:00。

- 人数：18 人。
- 设备：每位参加者必须携带能够使用现代网页浏览器的笔记本电脑。
- 费用：免费。
