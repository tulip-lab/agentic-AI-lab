# Agent Risk Register Template

**Language:** English | [中文版](risk-register-template-zh.md)

Record at least six risks that are specific to this agent and its case evidence.
Do not only copy generic risk names. Every row must explain how the risk can occur in this system and which test checks the control.

## Scale

Use low, medium, or high for likelihood and impact.
Residual risk is what remains after the current controls; do not assume it is “none”.
